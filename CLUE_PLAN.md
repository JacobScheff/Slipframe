# Environmental clues and art pass

Updated September 27: no humanoid phantom, projected hands, wrist marks or repeated character gestures. Clues belong to each environment's construction. Removed the large recurring emblems and the cross-biome display rack.

## Implemented details

- **Ember:** grounded basalt outcrops, mineral seams, volcanic fragments and new rock formations.
- **Summit:** snow-covered cliff ledges, small rear brackets, surface strata and new rock clusters.
- **Low Crawl:** cooling slots, fasteners, reinforced obstacle edges, cable runs and a briefly opening service hatch.
- **Ghost Glass:** fractured optical panes and mounts. Its gameplay walls retain near-invisible materials.
- **Storm:** fan and speaker hardware, rain nozzles, a drain strip and cooling seams. Fan and speaker motion respond to wind.
- **Crystal:** paired crystals in machined sockets, mineral detail, new cluster silhouettes and a brief signal along a side conduit after merging.
- **Lattice Forge:** a gantry, carriage, tooling plates, rails, vents and fasteners. Conduit lights respond when a shape door opens.
- **Gyre:** curved violet ring panels, recessed channels, fasteners, encoder ticks and clamps. Side indicators briefly respond to the first three successful passes.

## Variation and readability

Four wall variants per environment. Ember, Summit, Crystal and Gyre each have five randomized scenery variants. Other scenery counts are unchanged. Cosmetic selection remains independent of gameplay randomness.

Gyre no longer overrides authored materials with pink, yellow and cyan. Body, channel and trim materials remain separate. The timed opening has pale lavender edges.

## Review status

Blender source and game USDZ assets are saved. Review renders are in Art/Previews. Asset checks cover packages, mesh validity, triangle budgets, collision dimensions and scenery clearance. A visionOS/Xcode build and headset review are still required for scale, brightness, animation timing and performance.

The computer's identity, motivation and ending remain narrative proposals. This art pass does not implement story progression or an ending.
